      Spotted by the scout

      New candidates

      Every 3 hours the scout searches GitHub, GitLab, Hacker News, Bluesky, Mastodon, Lemmy and 4chan (/v/, /vg/) for new efforts. Repos with real code are listed here until they're reviewed. They are deliberately not linked, and copies of tracked projects are filtered out.

      There is no download

      No project on this page hosts the game, keys or an executable, and this site never links to any. Anything offering a ready-to-play GTA VI for PC this week is a scam.

        Methodology

        How the progress bar works

        One ladder for every project. Groundwork fills the first 25%: the parts that can be built before the executable exists. After that, a project only moves by reaching real milestones in GTA VI itself.

          Groundwork, weighted

            Past "In-game", the last 30 points split between frame rate (average FPS ÷ 30) and stability (from crashes often up to stable). Partial milestones, such as a menu that renders but is broken, count half.

            Maintain a project and think it's mis-scored? Open an issue with a link to your evidence.
